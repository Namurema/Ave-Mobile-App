# Ave illustrations: AI image prompts

One illustration per prayer, all in the same style as the "How to Pray: The Lord's Prayer" image: flat, bold African Catholic art, gold halos, kente patterns, deep navy backgrounds.

## How to use

1. Paste the **style block** below, then the **subject** for the image you want, into the image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Ideogram or Midjourney all work).
2. Ask for a **landscape image, 3:2, at least 1536 x 1024**. Keep the main figure in the **middle third**: the app also cuts it into a square for some cards.
3. **No words in the picture.** The app adds the titles itself, in every language.
4. Before you save it, check it closely (see "Check before you use it").
5. Save it as the file name given, and put it in `assets/illustrations/`. Then tell me, and I'll compress it and put it in the app.

## Style block (paste this first, every time)

> Flat vector illustration in a bold, warm African Catholic art style. Rich deep navy blue background (#0B1E5B). Figures with dark brown skin, gentle closed or downcast eyes, peaceful expressions, rendered with clean shapes and soft painterly shading. Gold circular halos on saints. Kente-inspired woven patterns in orange, yellow, red, green and black on clothing or as decorative bands at the sides. Sky-blue circular backdrop behind the main figure, with small silhouettes of an East African church with a cross and an acacia tree. Reverent, joyful, dignified. Crisp edges, subtle canvas texture, high detail. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Landscape 3:2.

## Check before you use it

AI image tools often get religious details wrong. Look for:

- **The rosary:** five groups of ten beads with one bead between groups, and a crucifix (not a plain cross) at the end.
- **Halos:** only on saints, Our Lady and Jesus, never on the ordinary people praying.
- **Hands and fingers:** five fingers on each hand, and joined hands that look natural.
- **No stray text or squiggly letters** anywhere in the picture.
- **Respect:** Jesus, Mary and the saints shown reverently, nothing cartoonish or silly.

If something is wrong, ask the tool to "fix only the …" (for example "fix only the rosary beads"), or generate it again.
